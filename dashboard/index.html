System Documentation

Risk Scoring Algorithm

The system uses a transparent, weighted scoring model:

  • Academic Performance (40%) - Based on subject averages
  • Attendance Rate (35%) - Percentage of days present
  • Fee Payment (15%) - Percentage of fees paid
  • Declining Trend (10%) - Performance drop between terms

Limitation: This model does not account for home environment, health, or school climate factors.

Interpretation Guidelines

High Risk (≥70)

Suggest: Parent meeting, academic support plan, financial counseling, attendance contract

Medium Risk (45-69)

Suggest: Monitor weekly, send progress reports, targeted tutoring

Low Risk (<45)

Suggest: Continue standard tracking and encouragement

Limitations & Ethics

This system has important limitations:

  • No validation studies have been conducted
  • May reflect historical biases in training data
  • Does not measure protective factors (family support, motivation)
  • Should never be the sole basis for educational decisions

Recommended practice: Use as a screening tool, not a diagnostic device.
